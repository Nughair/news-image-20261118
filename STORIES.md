# The stories in the picture

Every event below happened, or was first reported, on **Wednesday
18 November 2026**. The picture had room for six stories plus a ticker;
the selection favours consequence and breadth — three continents of
politics and war, plus health, weather and markets — over any single
country or beat.

## Lead — Shenzhen, China (diplomacy)

Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin met in Shenzhen on Wednesday, and Donald
Trump arrived for the APEC gathering, ahead of a planned three-way
summit of the American, Chinese and Russian leaders. Ukraine, Iran and
trade were expected to head the agenda. *Reported by AP and AFP.*

## Conflict — the Gulf (Middle East)

The United States struck a Revolutionary Guard missile site on Iran's
Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz; Iran answered with strikes on
Kuwait's Ali Al Salem Air Base and on Bahrain, its third attack there
in under a week. *Reported by Reuters, the Wall Street Journal, CBS
News and Al Jazeera.*

## War — Crimea (Europe)

Ukraine struck the Kerch Bridge between Russia and occupied Crimea
for a second straight day, and part of its road span fell into the
strait. *Reported across four outlets in the day's news digests.*

## Politics — United States

Democrat Mary Peltola defeated Republican incumbent Dan Sullivan in
Alaska, giving the Democrats a projected 56–44 majority in the
incoming U.S. Senate. *Called by the Associated Press.*

## Health — DR Congo (Africa)

The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has more
than doubled in three months, passing 11,000 cases and 5,400 deaths,
the UN said; children under five are dying at the highest rate.
*Reported from UN figures in the day's digests.*

## Weather — Cook Islands (Pacific)

Category 5 Cyclone Xavier made landfall in the Northern Cook Islands.
*Reported in the day's world news digests.*
