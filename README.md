One image that sums up the world news of Wednesday 18 November 2026, drawn as a newspaper front page: a masthead naming the day, a lead panel on the Shenzhen summit of Xi, Putin and Trump, panels on the U.S. strike on Iran's Qeshm Island and Iran's answer in Bahrain and Kuwait, Ukraine's second strike on the Crimea Bridge, the Democrats' 56–44 U.S. Senate win, the doubling Ebola outbreak in DR Congo and Category 5 Cyclone Xavier in the Cook Islands, and a ticker with the day's market slide, the U.S. losing its measles-free status, Nvidia's doubled revenue and the Siberia plane crash. The picture is drawn by `draw.py` (flat pictograms and bold short labels, so it stays legible small); `STORIES.md` lists every story with its sources. The entry program `main.py` copies the finished `entry.png` to the output directory, byte for byte.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
# copies entry.png into out/
```
